REVIEW CONTENT
================================================================================

This paper proposes a two-level optimization framework for joint access-point (AP) selection and precoding in cell-free massive MIMO systems. The core idea of using a Quadratic Unconstrained Binary Optimization (QUBO) formulation for the discrete selection task, solved by an Ising-inspired annealer, and a regularized zero-forcing (RZF) precoder for the continuous task is novel and timely. However, the manuscript in its current form is severely underdeveloped and falls far short of the standards for a top-tier publication. It reads more like a preliminary research summary than a complete paper.

**Major Weaknesses:**

1.  **Insufficient Depth and Content:** The paper is critically short, with most sections being mere outlines. Key areas like the Introduction, Related Work, QUBO Formulation, and Results lack the necessary detail for a reader to understand, critique, or reproduce the work. The total word count is far below the expected length for a full research paper. For instance, the "Performance Analysis" subsection under Results is just two sentences long and presents no actual data or figures.

2.  **Structural and LaTeX Errors:** The LaTeX source contains critical structural errors. A `\usepackage` command is erroneously placed inside a bibliography entry, and an orphaned `\item` appears before `\begin{document}`. These issues prevent proper compilation and indicate a lack of careful preparation. Furthermore, several references listed in the bibliography are not cited in the text, violating standard academic practice.

3.  **Lack of Rigorous Evaluation:** The paper describes a simulation setup but presents no results. There are no figures, tables, or quantitative data to support the claims made in the abstract and introduction. A rigorous empirical evaluation is the cornerstone of a systems paper. This would require:
    *   **Well-Defined Baselines:** The baselines are listed but not described in sufficient detail. Their implementation and rationale must be clarified.
    *   **Quantitative Results:** Performance metrics (sum rate, fairness) must be plotted against system parameters (e.g., number of users) in figures.
    *   **Statistical Analysis:** Results must be averaged over numerous random initializations (seeds), and statistical significance should be established using confidence intervals (visualized as error bars) and, where appropriate, statistical tests (e.g., paired t-tests).
    *   **Ablation Studies:** The sensitivity of the QUBO model to its hyperparameters (e.g., penalty weights $\lambda$) and the impact of the fairness weighting scheme should be systematically investigated.

4.  **Incomplete and Buggy Simulation Code:** The provided `simulation.py` file is non-functional. The `main` function is a placeholder that calls a non-existent function (`solve_qubo_selection`) and does not implement the experimental loop described in the paper. The `cim_anneal` function is truncated. Such incomplete code makes reproducibility impossible and casts doubt on the validity of any preliminary results the authors may have.

5.  **Superficial Discussion:** The "Discussion and Limitations" section consists of a bulleted list of topics without any substantive analysis. A thorough discussion should explore the practical implications of the findings, honestly address the method's limitations (e.g., CSI acquisition overhead, scalability of the dense QUBO, fidelity of the interference approximation), and propose concrete directions for future research.

**Recommendation: Major Revision**

While the paper is not acceptable for publication in its current state, the core concept is promising. A complete overhaul is required. The authors must:
1.  **Restructure and Expand:** Rewrite the paper from the ground up, expanding every section with detailed explanations, mathematical derivations, and clear justifications for design choices. The final manuscript should be 5000-8000 words.
2.  **Fix LaTeX and Citations:** Correct all LaTeX structural errors and ensure every reference in the bibliography is cited appropriately in the text.
3.  **Implement a Complete Simulation:** Provide a complete, runnable, and well-documented simulation script that implements the proposed method and all baselines.
4.  **Conduct and Present a Rigorous Evaluation:** Generate figures and tables summarizing the results of a comprehensive experimental study. All figures must include error bars, and the text must discuss the statistical significance of the findings.
5.  **Deepen the Analysis:** Provide a thorough complexity analysis and an expanded discussion of limitations and future work.

This revision will require a substantial effort, but it is necessary to transform the current draft into a high-quality research contribution.
