It uses a rotation–entangler–rotation ansatz, parameter-shift gradients, and a NumPy state-vector simulator to show convergence to the optimal cut with strong probability concentration on the four optimal bitstrings. The work situates itself within NISQ-era algorithm–hardware co-design and provides baseline comparisons and ablations.
