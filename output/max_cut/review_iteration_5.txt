Summary
The manuscript presents a hardware-matched variational quantum circuit (VQC) for Max-Cut, demonstrated on a five-qubit instance suitable for trapped-ion processors. It employs a rotation–entangler–rotation ansatz, parameter-shift training, and a NumPy-based state-vector simulator. The paper reports convergence to the theoretical maximum cut value with high probability mass on the four optimal bitstrings, and includes classical baselines and a QAOA-p=1 comparator. It argues for algorithm–hardware co-design in the NISQ era and attempts to provide a reproducible evaluation.

Major strengths
- Hardware-aware ansatz design aligned with trapped-ion capabilities and constraints.
- Diagonal cost measurement strategy that avoids basis rotations and reduces sampling overhead.
- Clear methodology including parameter-shift gradients and a simple, reproducible simulator artifact.
- Inclusion of baselines, ablations, complexity and sampling analysis, and explicit limitations.
- Authentic references across VQAs, QAOA, ion-trap hardware, and measurement optimization.
