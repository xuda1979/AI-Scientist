The manuscript addresses a timely and practically relevant problem: dynamic, traffic-aware tuning of Massive MIMO parameters (active antenna count, vertical downtilt, pilot reuse) across multiple cells. The core idea is to cast the multi-parameter, multi-cell configuration task as a Quadratic Unconstrained Binary Optimization (QUBO) problem whose coefficients encode local utilities and interference-driven pairwise couplings, then solve it with a physics-inspired heuristic akin to simulated annealing or coherent Ising machines. This is a strong and interesting direction with potential impact on self-organizing networks (SON) and automated RAN optimization.
