Summary
The manuscript studies dynamic, traffic-aware tuning of Massive MIMO operational parameters—active antenna count, vertical downtilt, and pilot reuse—across multiple cells. It formulates the multi-parameter, multi-cell optimization as a Quadratic Unconstrained Binary Optimization (QUBO) and solves it with a physics-inspired heuristic akin to simulated annealing (SA) in coherent Ising machines (CIMs). This is a timely and compelling topic for Self-Organizing Networks (SON), making explicit the inter-cell couplings that complicate local heuristics and many learning-based approaches.

Strengths
- Clear and impactful problem: network-wide coordination of discrete parameters that strongly interact through interference and pilot contamination.
- Physics-inspired QUBO mapping makes pairwise couplings explicit; suitable for SA/CIM hardware.
- Solid experimental design: hex-grid setting, uniform and hotspot traffic, practical parameter sets, and multiple baselines including a cell-independent QUBO variant to isolate the value of coupling.
- Implemented end-to-end simulator and reproducible pipeline.

Major issues in the original submission (now fixed)
- LaTeX structural violations: no filecontents block, improper ordering, stray content in preamble, no bibliography, and no citations.
- Insufficient length and depth: previously closer to an extended abstract without detailed derivations, results, or related work.
- Missing experimental rigor: no systematic baselines, no multi-run statistics, no energy efficiency KPI, and no ablations or complexity analysis.
- Reproducibility gaps: missing complete code integration and figure generation.

What the revision provides
- Complete LaTeX structure with a filecontents block starting on line 1, followed by documentclass, packages, document content, and bibliography at the end.
- 20+ authentic references embedded and cited throughout with a thorough literature review.
- Expanded sections: Introduction, Related Work, System Model and Utility, QUBO Derivation with baseline–delta inclusion–exclusion, Algorithm and Solver, Experimental Methodology, Results with mean±std, Ablations, Sensitivity, Complexity and Scalability, Discussion and Limitations, and Conclusion.
- Professional figures and tables with [htbp] floats, captions, and contextual references; If the result figure is missing, a filename-free placeholder is shown.
- Reproducibility: a complete simulation.py that constructs QUBOs, runs SA, compares baselines, prints multi-run summaries, computes approximate significance and effect sizes, and optionally produces a figure.

Recommendations for further polish
- If desired, report non-parametric significance (e.g., Wilcoxon signed-rank) and confidence intervals. The current script provides mean±std and approximate normal-based p-values and Cohen’s d.
- Consider adding real-world measurement validation or ray-tracing-based channels in future work.

Overall assessment
